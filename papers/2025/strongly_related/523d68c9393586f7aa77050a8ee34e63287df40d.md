## abstract

While most adversarial attacks are designed in image recognition, adversarial robustness in 3D action recognition is important to the real-world applications of healthcare, robotics, and surveillance. This paper reports the adversarial robustness achieved by a hybrid Spatiotemporal Graph Convolution Network, which can capture complex human motion dynamics with enhanced adversarial robustness. The model in this work is performed for the development of superior feature engineering techniques that take into consideration the pairwise joint distances and augmented angular velocities to further improve the clarity of motion patterns. This was followed by comprehensive adversarial training that provided robustness on noisy and perturbed inputs, hence making it perform excellently under challenging environments. The performance achieved in the proposed approach was 70% accuracy on the MHAD dataset, which proved to be much more robust and adaptable when compared to other state-of-the-art approaches. Such contributions to the field of action recognition offer an avenue toward much more reliable and accurate solutions for critical applications.
