## abstract

Summation algorithms with great compactness and contextual richness are needed to process rapidly rising surveillance, autonomous and digital media video data. Existing approaches are mostly limited to scene-based or shot-based condensation, overlooking fine-grained object interactions that respect temporal causality sets. Such inadequacies cause summaries to be shorter in visual appearance but shallow in semantics, lacking in interpretability and analytical depth $h$. In an attempt to circumvent these inadequacies, we offer a Hierarchical Context-Aware Summarization framework that integrates object detection, spatio-temporal tracking and contextual reasoning into one complete pipeline. The system employs five novel analytical processes: (i) Context-Aware Multi-Scale Object Graph Transformer for encoding spatial relations across local and global object ierarchies, (ii) Temporal Causality Preserving Recurrent Memory Unit to retain action continuity through explicit cause-effect modeling, (iii) Hierarchical Attention Fusion Summarizer for multi-level alignment of object, scene and process information, (iv) Spatio-Temporal Contrastive Learning with Context Augmentation to enhance robustness against noise and occlusion and (v) Generative Event Abstraction Network for producing multimodal outputs in symbolic event-graph and natural language forms. To ensure semantically profound and analytically verifiable summaries, the settings were optimized for spatial coherence, temporal fidelity, information density, resilience or interpretability. The recommended solution advances the field using context-preserving hierarchical abstractions instead of shot compression. Experimental results show improved contextual recall, causal continuity, resilience and explainability, culminating in short, scientifically analyzable video summaries for intelligent decision-making.
