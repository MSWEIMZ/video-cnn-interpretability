## abstract

The study of Human Activity Recognition (HAR) has attracted considerable attention lately driven by its expanding applications in diverse fields. Advances in deep learning, particularly Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), have revolutionized HAR from video data sources. In particular, deep residual networks (ResNet) have demonstrated strong performance across various tasks. The purpose of this research is to evaluate the performance of CNNs with spatiotemporal three-dimensional (3D) Kernels, employing finetuning techniques for action recognition. In our approach, we employ a three-dimensional residual neural network (ResNet50) to extract features, classify each sample with its respective label, and identify the executed activity. We trained and tested the model using the MSR Daily Activity 3D dataset, with additional benchmarking conducted on other datasets like HMDB51 and UCF101 to assess its performance. The experimental results indicate that our model performs competitively with state-of-the-art approaches.
