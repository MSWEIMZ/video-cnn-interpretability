## abstract

Action Quality Assessment (AQA) requires capturing subtle execution differences and understanding fine-grained sub action transitions. Existing visual-only models struggle to generalize across action styles due to the lack of semantic context and poor interpretability. This paper presents a hybrid visual-semantic model that integrates a Temporal Parsing Transformer (TPT) with semantic embeddings obtained from a pretrained BERT encoder. The TPT decomposes video sequences into ordered sub actions through learnable queries, enabling detailed temporal understanding without manual annotations. A Global Alignment Module further aligns parsed visual features with semantic descriptions using mutual-information-based optimization. Experiments on the MTL-AQA dataset demonstrate that the proposed framework achieves superior score prediction and ranking consistency compared with existing state-of-the-art methods. The results confirm that combining temporal parsing with semantic guidance enhances robustness, interpretability, and overall AQA performance.
