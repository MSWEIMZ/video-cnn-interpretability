## abstract

Real-time and accurate detection of abnormal behaviors, such as violent conflicts and falls, in crowded scenarios is critical to public safety. However, existing single-frame detectors lack temporal modeling capability, while video-based networks often require high computational resources. To address these challenges, this paper introduces a lightweight spatiotemporal feature enhancement module, termed TemporalGRU-FusedDown. The module consists of two components: TemporalGRU, embedded in the low-level feature pyramid (P3), aggregates features from short temporal windows via gated recurrent units (GRU) to explicitly encode motion cues; and FusedDown, which integrates channel expansion with down-sampling to efficiently align and propagate the enhanced low-level features to higher layers. This plug-and-play design can be seamlessly integrated into existing detection frameworks. On the RT-DETR baseline, the proposed module achieves an absolute improvement of 5.22 percentage points in mAP@50 (equivalent to a relative gain of approximately 6.7%), with only about 3.4% reduction in inference speed, along with notable improvements in both precision and recall. Ablation studies and visualization further demonstrate that the two components complement each other by suppressing cross-frame jitter, enhancing stability in detecting short-duration and occluded targets, and achieving a balanced trade-off among accuracy, efficiency, and robustness. These results highlight the potential of the proposed method for practical deployment.
